01 / Overview

Understand the story behind fuel prices.

Fuel costs affect household budgets, transport networks and national energy policy. This project brings several related factors into one visual analysis.

Project overview

The project uses a global fuel-price dataset to compare petrol, diesel and LPG prices across countries and regions. It also explores tax percentages, subsidy categories, income levels and changes over time through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

Fuel price comparisonRegional analysisTaxationSubsidies

Key objectives

  • ✓Compare fuel prices across countries and regions.
  • ✓Explore taxation and subsidy patterns.
  • ✓Track changes in fuel prices over time.
  • ✓Investigate relationships between prices, taxes and income levels.
